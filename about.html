About

Hi, I'm Bogdan.

I'm a self-taught developer based in Gdańsk, Poland, currently studying while building Northloop. I got into programming by building things I actually wanted to exist — a trip-planning bot, small automations, layouts for real local businesses — and Northloop is the next step: turning that into a studio that takes on outside work properly.

Right now

One person, full attention.
